Membership Plans

PROFESSIONAL

Designed for busy executives and high-performers. Executive locker access, flexible 24/7 priority scheduling, and monthly bespoke programming.

ELITE

Built for serious lifters pushing physical limits. Unlimited 24/7 access, full recovery suite usage, and quarterly 1-on-1 performance assessments.

INTERMEDIATE

Standard open-gym access during all operational hours plus 4 specialty group training sessions per month to keep your progress moving.

REGULAR

Essential open-gym access during off-peak hours. Ideal for consistent lifters who prefer training on a quieter gym floor.

STARTER

Budget-friendly option tailored for students and beginners looking to build a consistent training routine without long commitments.

SEASONAL

Short-term flexibility for visiting athletes and travelers. Pay week-to-week with zero sign-up fees or cancellation contracts.
